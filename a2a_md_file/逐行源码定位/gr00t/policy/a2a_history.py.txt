00016 | """Actual-proprio history buffering for action-to-action inference.
00017 | 
00018 | The A2A source trajectory must describe what the robot actually executed.  This
00019 | module therefore deliberately has no API for actions predicted or commanded by
00020 | the policy.  Callers must feed measured proprioception from robot/environment
00021 | observations through :meth:`A2AProprioHistoryBuffer.append_actual` or
00022 | :meth:`A2AProprioHistoryBuffer.resolve`.
00023 | 
00024 | All public histories use ``[batch, horizon, feature]`` layout.  The buffer is
00025 | model-independent and keeps each state key separate so a later processor can
00026 | perform embodiment-specific canonicalization.
00027 | """
00028 | 
00029 | from __future__ import annotations

00039 | TimestampLike: TypeAlias = float | int | Sequence[float] | np.ndarray
00040 |

00042 | @dataclass(frozen=True)
00043 | class A2AHistoryBatch:
00044 |     """A fixed-horizon batch of measured proprioception.
00045 | 
00046 |     Attributes:
00047 |         states: State arrays in ``[B, H, D_key]`` layout.  Arrays are defensive
00048 |             copies and may be safely modified by a downstream processor.
00049 |         timestamps: Per-batch timestamps in ``[B, H]`` layout.  Cold-start
00050 |             padding repeats the first real timestamp, just as it repeats the
00051 |             first real state.
00052 |         valid_mask: Boolean ``[B, H]`` mask.  ``False`` marks synthetic
00053 |             cold-start positions; external and full-buffer histories are all
00054 |             valid.
00055 |         metadata: Integration-friendly source and shape information.  In
00056 |             particular, ``metadata["source"]`` identifies whether the result
00057 |             came from an external history, a full ring buffer, or
00058 |             ``repeat_first_state`` cold-start padding.
00059 |     """
00060 | 
00061 |     states: dict[str, np.ndarray]

00063 |     valid_mask: np.ndarray
00064 |     metadata: dict[str, Any]

00066 | 
00067 | class A2AProprioHistoryBuffer:
00068 |     """Fixed-horizon ring buffer containing only measured proprioception.
00069 | 
00070 |     Args:
00071 |         horizon: Number of historical samples returned to the A2A encoder.
00072 |         state_keys: Optional exact set/order of state keys.  If omitted, the
00073 |             first measured sample or external history establishes the schema.
00074 |         max_time_gap_s: Optional maximum allowed interval between consecutive
00075 |             measured samples.  Timestamps are always required, finite, and
00076 |             strictly increasing even when this limit is disabled.
00077 | 
00078 |     Notes:
00079 |         ``reset()`` starts a new episode and clears samples and timestamps while
00080 |         preserving the established key/feature schema.  Batch size may change
00081 |         after a reset.
00082 | 
00083 |         An external history is treated as an authoritative history of *actual
00084 |         measured proprioception*.  It takes priority over a simultaneously
00085 |         supplied current sample and re-seeds the local ring buffer, allowing a
00086 |         later local sample to continue from it.

00220 |         """Validate and install an authoritative full measured history.
00221 |

00223 |         key in ``[B, H, D]`` layout.  ``timestamps`` must be ``[H]`` (shared by
